Our Story

A Chennai institute built one practical class at a time

CSC Computer Software College is an ISO 9001:2015 certified computer education institute, part of a long-running training network that has grown from a single centre into hundreds of branches across Tamil Nadu — including our neighbourhood centre right here in Pammal.

The idea has stayed the same throughout: teach computers the way people actually use them at work. That means project-based training, updated syllabi (our 2026 prospectus adds AI & Data Science, Cloud Computing and Cybersecurity tracks alongside the classic Tally and MS-Office programs), and faculty who stay in the lab until your doubt is actually cleared — not just until the clock runs out.

Our curriculum is designed and reviewed by an ISO-certified committee that pulls input from industry, past students and academic partners, so what you learn in the Pammal lab maps to what employers ask for in interviews.

Do I need any computer knowledge to join?+

No. Most of our programs, including DMO, DCA and HDCA, are designed for absolute beginners — school-final students, homemakers, graduates and working professionals all join with zero prior experience.

The fees and durations above are from our current 2026 prospectus. Batch timing, combo discounts and any seasonal offers are best confirmed with our Pammal centre directly on a quick call or WhatsApp.

Do you help with placements?+

Every diploma is project-based so you finish with something concrete to show employers, and several of our Pammal students mention in their reviews that the practical training here helped them get hired.
